**Acceptance criteria.**

  policy, full compilation shall emit Claude prompt/tool/status advice by default,
  with declared spend, sessions and caps, without fabricating permissions or gates.
- When restart block is explicit, only advisory Stop shall be added; verification

  before settings, persona or guidance writes, preserving all existing bytes.
- When intact legacy cap-only settings migrate, their shape and source/count data
  shall be validated; removing a cap shall keep default advice. Missing manifests
  and pointers shall neither adopt nor remove new managed settings.
- When portable exemplars are compared through the guidance-only API, independent
  root settings, root persona and both guidance drift controls shall stay red;
  normal policy-backed settings shall retain their exact generated bytes.
