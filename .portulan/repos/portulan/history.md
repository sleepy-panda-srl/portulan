_(**The counts that used to stand here are deleted rather than corrected a sixth time**, 2026-08-25, when clause (b) added two recipes. They had been wrong **five** times: seven-and-eight after `control-chars`, again after composition, nine-and-ten after `rule-carriers`, and still reading nine-and-eleven against a measured fourteen-and-fifteen when milestone 8 session 0 opened — a five-recipe drift accumulated unnoticed. Caught by a reader every time, because `doctor`'s claims lint reads the path-shaped tokens on the test line and never the numbers around them. The recipe-name roster went the same way on 2026-08-24 for the same reason; this is that repair finished. A hand-maintained figure whose subject grows is a carrier that fails silently, and the command [on that line](../portulan.md) is the one that cannot.)_
