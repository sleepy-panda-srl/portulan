## The shape of the job, and why it is the inverse of a known one

Seven days ago [`2026-08-10-the-flip-back-left-its-carriers-standing.md`](2026-08-10-the-flip-back-left-its-carriers-standing.md)
ran this task in the other direction: the flip back to private minted twenty-nine false present-tense
claims at a single instant. This session runs it forward, and the asymmetry is the whole difficulty —
**a sweep after a flip corrects sentences that are already false; a sweep before one writes sentences
that are false until the moment they are not.** So the text here is written to be true the instant the
repository is public, the PR says merging it is part of the flip motion rather than a change standing on
its own, and the abort path is named: revert the merge if the flip does not follow.

The governing rule was inherited rather than re-derived — **state moves, identity stays**, and **dated
records are forward-only**. Thirty-three edited passages across twenty-three files, counted from the
commit's own diff rather than from the roster that found them.
