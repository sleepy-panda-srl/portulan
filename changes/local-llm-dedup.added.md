- **`dedup`, a screen of a curated slot for duplicate files, in the [`tools/local-llm`](../packs/tools/local-llm/README.md#screening-a-slot-for-duplicates) runner.**
  `local-llm.mjs dedup --dir <slot-dir> [--threshold 0.80]` embeds the slot's Markdown files, its `README.md` left out, through a loopback `/v1/embeddings` endpoint with the pack's embedding model, and prints each pair at or above the threshold by descending cosine similarity, one line each. It keeps the runner's rails: an endpoint off loopback, a redirect and a file over the per-file `--max-input-bytes` ceiling are refused before anything is embedded, and `runner-rails` now drills the first and last. Its ranking and threshold are tested offline on synthetic vectors in [`cli/pack-local-llm.test.mjs`](../cli/pack-local-llm.test.mjs).
