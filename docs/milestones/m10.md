## Decision — 2026-08-14, landed 2026-08-17 on the maintainer's instruction

> **Nothing in row 10 changes.** The row already owes a deliverable named in two words —

> supervisor on the maintainer's commission of 2026-08-14; **landed on his explicit wrap-up
> instruction of 2026-08-17**. The services shape is the decision of record; every figure stays
> indicative — first thoughts — until his pricing ruling, as [`../pricing.md`](../pricing.md)

**"Private-feed pricing" resolves to services pricing, and the premium-pack feed is explicitly
deferred.** The commercial offer at re-opening is two productized services around the free layer:
**Chartmaking** (the onboarding engagement — the customer's workspace authored with them and owned
by them outright, fixed price) and **Chart-keeping** (a curation retainer — the librarian and
supervisor roles staffed across the customer's boundary; their humans approve, per supervised
evolution). Draft prices and the full argument: [`../pricing.md`](../pricing.md), which is written
for this row's docs surface and marked DRAFT until ruled on.

**Grounds.** (1) The vision's commercial sentence puts customer workspaces before premium packs,
and storage-follows-ownership means a customer's feed lives in the customer's org — so there is no
standing Sleepy Panda catalog to subscribe to, and no maintainer for one: `packs/` ships two
public packs today, and the build's own record prices what keeping even those green costs. A
content subscription would be a promise with no funded keeper — the exact class of unfunded
mandate the framework exists to prevent. A pack is priced the day its upkeep is funded, not
before. (2) The row's "**buy** without talking to anyone" is satisfied by a fixed-price,
fixed-scope service bookable through a checkout — a purchase, not a sales call, and not a hosted
surface, so the no-SaaS non-goal is untouched. (3) The approval-relay clause of this row is not
touched by this note in either direction.

**Evidence the offer stands on** (measured 2026-08-14 on `c93a819`, this branch): the paid
journey's mechanical half runs end to end on a repository Portulan had never seen — `init` drafted
12 files and composed `rituals/checkpoints` by default, `doctor` graded the draft GREEN with 13
honest notes, `compile` wrote both backends' artifacts and named its refusals, `vendor --host`
rendered a self-contained `AGENTS.md` for a non-Claude host; the eleven yielded recipes ran green
by the CI loop's own carrier and the suite passed 1608 of 1608. What the offer therefore does not
claim: no premium catalog, no relay, no evals, no `npx` path (registry 404, publishing Gated), no
docs surface yet — each named as unbuilt where a buyer would otherwise assume it.
