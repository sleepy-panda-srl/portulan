## Nothing has been relocated here, and that is the record

**This milestone has no legislative history yet, and two dated rulings.** It has never been *worked*: no
session has implemented against it, its criterion has never been amended, and it has no close to carry
evidence for. So the post-M5 reconciliation had nothing to move out of row 9.

**But a session opened against it and was stopped, and it left two rulings behind.** On **2026-09-03** a
session planned against this row, took a REQUEST-CHANGES at its session-open — the criterion reads two
ways and is the maintainer's to disambiguate — and was then redirected by him to milestone 8. He ruled
the ambiguity on the way past, and the rulings are recorded here rather than only in
[`m08.md`](m08.md)'s session note, because a reader opening this row will not look there:

- **"Headless PR-as-gate recipe" means a demonstrated headless path** — a documented, demonstrated
  procedure by which an unattended run lands work through PR-as-gate, of the shape
  [`../../.github/workflows/librarian.yml`](../../.github/workflows/librarian.yml) already instantiates
  once and [`a-scheduled-agent-needs-its-own-identity`](../../.portulan/memory/a-scheduled-agent-needs-its-own-identity.md)
  generalises. It is **not** an offline verify recipe over the floor's in-tree preconditions — that
  reading was proposed and declined, and most of it is in any case already railed: `doctor` has failed a
  required status context that no workflow job reports since milestone 4.
- **The incidental question it raised, ruled the same day:** a gate policy carrying `propose` rules and
  **no `floor` block at all stays green**. `cli/compile.mjs` calls that a legitimate shape, and
  [#402](https://github.com/sleepy-panda-srl/portulan/issues/402) — which asks for the *accounting* to
  stop reading as coverage — deliberately adds **no declaration** that a floor is in force, so nothing
  in the tree would have a fact to fail on.

Neither is an amendment: the row's operative wording is untouched, and a criterion is amended by the
maintainer in [`../plan.md`](../plan.md) alone. They are the *reading* of wording that already stands.

**The file existed before either of them**, and the reason it did is the one the maintainer gave when
milestones 0 and 1 were given theirs: **a gap in this directory reads as an exemption.** A reader walking
the milestone table finds a file behind every other row, and the absence of one behind this row invites
the inference that some rows are exempt from carrying their history — which is exactly the reading the
split was designed to prevent at the Status column. Declaring the absence costs one file; leaving it to
be inferred costs the property.

That is the same discipline the verify recipes already practise on their own output: *nothing to
report* and *did not look* must never print the same way. An empty file here says **nothing to
report**. No file at all would have said neither. _(And this section is now the demonstration rather
than the argument: the file had somewhere to put a ruling on the day one arrived, which is the whole
case for writing it before there was anything to write in it.)_

## What will land here

The first session that opens against milestone 9 writes its session note here, not into the row —
[`../../.portulan/verify/docs.sh`](../../.portulan/verify/docs.sh)'s `plan` check refuses
`[Ss]ession N of` inside a milestone-table row, so the row cannot take it even by accident. Any
amendment to the criterion brings its argument and its expansion-or-narrowing verdict here, leaving
the operative wording in the row. The close leaves its evidence narrative here and a signed one-clause
verdict in Status.

Until then this page is correct as it stands, and it should not be padded to look busier than the
milestone is.
