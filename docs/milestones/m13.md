## Drafting rationale — 2026-09-23

**A new row, ruled by Marius, 2026-09-23: *"New row."*** The question offered a new row 13 or a wider row
12, and he chose the row. It is appended with nothing renumbered, the precedent rows 11 and 12 set. Its
place, after row 12 and before row 9, is carried by its Status cell and by the Sequencing rationale, not by
its number: it reuses row 12's measurement module and `doctor` report, and row 9 multiplies sessions, each
of which runs up the re-read cost this row prices.

**The other four questions he sent to the project's coordinator session**, to settle with the drafting
session on one criterion: *"what delivers the better performance enhacement"*. The settlements are in 0038;
two of them shaped this row directly:

- **The advisory is a report by default and a rail by declaration.** The threshold is an estimate, and a
  forced restart on a wrong estimate costs a fresh write of the whole context plus a handoff, while an
  ignored line costs nothing. That is 0036's line, *a defaulted rail is a non-goal; the default is the
  report*, applied once more.
- **The multipliers are declared.** The manifest carries them, as it carries bytes per token; `init` offers
  the host's own pricing where it exposes some, else a dated per-host table, else the general multipliers.
  Neither fallback performs on its own: the general multipliers restart too early on models with cheap
  reads, and a cheap-read fallback lets sessions on expensive-read models run long.

**The sessions column reads *1 per change*, not a total**, as row 12's does. Proposal 0038 orders its changes
one per pull request, and one of them, the per-host multiplier table, comes from a local session because it
names models.

**Three demonstrations, each chosen to prove a different thing is real:**

- **The ledger reproduces known totals and states its error.** A fixture of synthetic records with known
  totals, the per-block duplicates included, and a real session where the ledger's figures stand beside the
  host's own. A measurement that cannot state its error repeats the defect this proposal starts from: the
  host's summary reported four research readers at 811.7k tokens while they processed 15.05M.
- **The advisory appears once, at the computed threshold, and not before.** On a recorded session, the line
  appears at the request where the threshold is crossed and at no earlier one, and it prints the
  multipliers it assumed.
- **The shipped personas' routing arrives with its evidence.** Each of the three personas that run in fresh
  contexts carries an A/B record, and the routing Portulan ships is the one that record supports, arriving
  on upgrade with no configuration.

**What the row leaves out, on purpose:**

- **A batch-price rule.** No Portulan ritual calls a model API today, so the rule would have no instance and
  no owner.
- **The adoption in the sealed repository.** It is private, and a milestone's exit criterion must be
  demonstrable from this repository alone
  ([`a-public-criterion-must-be-demonstrable-from-this-repo`](../../.portulan/memory/a-public-criterion-must-be-demonstrable-from-this-repo.md)).
  It gates nothing here.
- **A version number.** The manifest keys ride the MINOR that 0036's key needs, which
  [`0034`](../../.portulan/proposals/0034-one-spec-bump-carries-both-evals-and-telemetry.md) commissioned and
  which is not drafted yet. The row names no version, so it cannot pre-empt that choice.
